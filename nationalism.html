> Need to know people & definitions
Johann Gottlieb Fichte

Known as one of the fathers of german nationalism, Fichte was an educator of german culture and language. He was a core proponent in "German Idealism" and sparked what would become nationalism accross europe.


Mazzini

Italian unificationism and founder of the "young italians" Guiseppe Mazinni was an Italian nationalist and politician that worked for the forming of one italian state. While it never happened under his jurisdiction, he did inspire and prophesize the idea.


Frederich Hegel

Hegel was a german philosipher and key figure in german idealism and epistemology. His critique of globalism and apologistic tendancies towards the prussian state is why scholars beliecve in Hegel being a nationalist


Reasons for nationalism

Napoleon played a large part in why nationalism became so prevalent in central and western europe, his colonization of europe and usurption of their leaders with french ones led to people feeling humiliated, a rise in the beleif that only those in their own community should lead them came up and stayed after he

German Nationalism

The invasion of the holy roman empire by Napoleon and after caused a sense of nationalistic pride to flourish in germany, especially via Fichte's writings and teachings that made german culture distinct and deserving of a state.

This sense of pride flourished during the romanticisjm era when people like the brothers grimm compiled a novel of german folk tales, which highlighted german history and traditions.



Methods

The idea behind nationalism is to ensure that a group of people has a state that they rule, therefore revolutions and unifications of nations without a state is a key component of nationalism.

However, nationalist doesnt have to be immediate action, the preserving of national heritage or traditions is also commonplace for nationalists, shown by the brothers grimm's fairy tales and Ficthe's teachings

"Onward to victory", a wwI nationalist postcard showing characters representing seperate nations, containing nationalistic identifier like flags, military colors, and cultural clothing to give the idea of nations coinciding with the people who rule them



Ethnic Nationalism

Ethnonationalism is the form of nationalism that beleives a nation is the ethnicity that ruels them, that nations and their people are defined by a common heritage, and that other ethnicities can possibly be considered "Second class" to those who are native.


Civic nationalism

Civic nationalism is less ethnocentric than ethnic nationalism, it preserves the ideas of freedom, equality, and individual rights, with a nation being defined as an identity that unites the people. Often buolt around shared citizenship rather than shared heritage
